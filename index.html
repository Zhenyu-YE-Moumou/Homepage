About

My name is Zhenyu YE.

I am a second-year PhD student in mathematics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Conan Leung. My research interests include quantization and coisotropic A-branes. I am also interested in Higgs bundles and related topics.

I received my MPhil from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2025, under the supervision of Conan Leung, and my bachelor's degree from China University of Mining and Technology, Beijing, in 2023.

Research interests

  • Deformation quantization
  • Mirror Symmetry
  • Higgs bundles
